As I originally started to piece together this website, I went looking for photos available at random on my laptop to use as placeholders. Given my poor file management skills, I like to think of my computer as a perpetual scavenger hunt. My favorite of these savagable digital objects are my family photos. Scattered haphazardly between a shared family icloud account and loose jpgs garnered from text messages, I wanted to use this opportunity to gather some of them into a visual collection. Hopefully the constraints of this assignment has given them some meaningful format beyond their obscure existence within our personal collection.

Some are faintly reminiscent of the common desire to document pithy monuments to history.
